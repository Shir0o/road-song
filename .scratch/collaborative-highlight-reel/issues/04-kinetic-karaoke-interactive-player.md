# 04: Kinetic Karaoke Subtitle & Interactive Video Player

**What to build:** 
An interactive 60fps Flutter player that renders kinetic Neo-Brutalist subtitles (words bouncing, shaking, highlighting in sync with vocal syllables) and automatically cuts between session media clips on musical beats during real-time playback, with full play/pause/seek interactive controls.

**Blocked by:** 03: Vocal Song Generation & SongTimeline Beat Alignment

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Neo-Brutalist kinetic subtitle CustomPainter / AnimatedWidget renderer supporting kinetic styles (bounce, shake, highlight).
- [ ] Beat-synced media switcher displaying session photos/videos aligned to downbeats.
- [ ] Interactive playback controls (play, pause, scrub, timecode display) with instant sync.
- [ ] Widget and unit test suite verifying subtitle and media frame calculation at arbitrary timestamps.
