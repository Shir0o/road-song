# 05: Define Phase 1 MVP scope

Type: grilling

## Question

What exactly is in and out of the Phase 1 MVP?

Decide:
- Which of the five user-journey steps ship in Phase 1 (ingestion, story prompts, lyric sandbox, vibe audition, turnkey export).
- Minimum media types and count (photos only? video clips?).
- Minimum lyric editing depth (line editing only, or syllable locking too?).
- Export resolution (720p only?) and branding requirements.
- Which pipeline stages can be stubbed vs fully integrated.
