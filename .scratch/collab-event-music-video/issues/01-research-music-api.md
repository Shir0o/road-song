# 01: Choose the music & vocal synthesis API

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by:

## Question

Which music/vocal synthesis provider should the platform standardize on for the MVP: ElevenLabs Music API, a Suno wrapper, or another option?

Decide based on:
- Full vocal + instrumental master track quality for ~2 minute songs.
- 15-second genre/melody previews for vibe audition.
- Section-level audio inpainting (free tier gives 2 per Trip Pass).
- API maturity, latency, cost per generated track, and licensing terms.

## Notes

- The brief estimates $0.15–$0.25 per full vocal master track.
- This decision blocks vibe audition and the export pipeline.
